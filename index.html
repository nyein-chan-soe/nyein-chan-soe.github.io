ABOUT

About me

I am a chemist and environmental researcher from Myanmar with an interdisciplinary academic background in Chemistry and Development Studies.

I hold a B.Sc. in Chemistry from West Yangon University and a Postgraduate Diploma in Applied Psychology from the University of Yangon. I am currently pursuing a Master of Development Studies at Yangon University of Economics.

My professional experience in environmental consulting has provided practical experience in environmental assessment, field monitoring, environmental quality measurements, sample management, data analysis, and technical reporting.

My long-term goal is to develop as a laboratory-based environmental chemist and conduct scientific research on chemical contaminants and their environmental and biological impacts, particularly in water systems.

CURRENT RESEARCH DIRECTION

PROPOSED GRADUATE RESEARCH

Fate, Transformation, and Biological Activity of Acetamiprid During Chlorination for Drinking Water Treatment

This research direction focuses on the fate and transformation of acetamiprid during drinking-water chlorination.

Key areas include chlorination kinetics, effects of pH and water matrices, transformation products, and their potential biological activity.

Potential analytical approaches include LC-MS/MS and high-resolution mass spectrometry.
